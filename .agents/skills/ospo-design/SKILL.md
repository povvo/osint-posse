# osint posse Router

This skill applies the osint posse design language: carbon-majority fields, silhouette-first recognition, unequal ember and mineral light, scarce pale convergence, particulate edge dissolution, singular composition, and stillness-led interaction. The package root is the active skill folder. Root `SKILL.md` sits beside root `DESIGN.md`.

## Procedure

1. Read `DESIGN.md` first before creating, reviewing, or repairing an output.
2. Name the artifact, audience, channel, platform, domain, ratio, required modes, and accessibility target.
3. Choose one canonical pattern and one governing axis. Prove subject count, relationship, and silhouette in greyscale before colour or texture.
4. Load only the Support Files required for the task.
5. Build with semantic and component tokens. Preserve complete states, native platform behaviour, recovery, reduced-motion parity, and information clarity.
6. For generated media, use the prompt contract and vary one controlled axis at a time. Reject extra subjects, vague mood-only prompts, pseudo-text, uniform glow, and generic cinematic grading.
7. Inspect at thumbnail, ordinary viewing size, and close range. Also inspect greyscale, high contrast, text scaling, keyboard or touch operation, responsive crops, and final encoding as applicable.
8. Request the external extraction metadata when readiness, drift, traceability, rights, or risk is at issue.

## Support Files

- `DESIGN.md`: primary source-free reconstruction and Use Contract.
- `foundations/`: visual DNA, seven-domain rules, imagery, voice, and expression.
- `tokens/`: primitive through platform tokens, transforms, and drift tests.
- `components/`: action, input, navigation, sequence, feedback, content, and domain-module contracts.
- `patterns/`: behavioural, perceptual, compositional, domain, and platform patterns.
- `accessibility/`: contrast, colour-vision, keyboard, focus, motion, media, and target obligations.
- `applications/`: intensity, placement, composition, and use-case rules.
- `outputs/`: documentation context, AI-readiness, and prompt contracts.

## Guardrails

- `DESIGN.md` is the creation contract. Do not substitute proof rationale for design guidance.
- Preserve the core grammar before applying a bounded narrative module or a controlled subversion.
- Do not treat hats, weapons, animals, shelters, bottles, vehicles, plants, settlements, or terrain as mandatory identity markers.
- Do not copy attached pixels into production work without confirmed permission.
- Do not invent raw values where tokens exist, unregistered components, unsupported variants, missing states, decorative motion, or prompt facts outside the contract.
- Keep live text, controls, focus, status, consent, data, and recovery crisp and free of grain, glow, clipping, and atmospheric obstruction.
- Mark an unsupported area as a gap and use platform-native behaviour rather than guessing.

## Output Contract

Return the created or reviewed artifact, the `DESIGN.md` rules applied, the Support Files consulted, validation or inspection performed, unresolved gaps, and residual risks. When reviewing, distinguish language fidelity from usability, accessibility, platform correctness, cultural safety, and rights readiness.
