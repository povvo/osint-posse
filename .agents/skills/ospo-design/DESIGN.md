# Emberglass Nocturne

## Use Contract

Emberglass Nocturne is a source-free design language for nocturnal editorial imagery, identity plates, narrative transitions, and restrained product surfaces. It is built from an absorptive dark field, silhouette-first recognition, unequal ember/mineral illumination, scarce pale convergence light, particulate edge dissolution, singular composition, and stillness-led interaction.

Designers, engineers, content teams, and generation agents may build from this manual without reopening any reference material. Use it to construct new imagery, responsive compositions, prompts, tokens, components, and accessible product expressions. It does not define a company strategy, logo, historical period, cultural identity, or product domain. Those require separate briefs and review.

Non-negotiables:

- Begin identity-bearing work with a near-black structural field; dark grey alone is insufficient.
- Keep dark plus charcoal at roughly `50–65%` of an ordinary identity frame.
- Use both ember-orange and mineral-cyan families, with visibly unequal area and chroma.
- Preserve a dark opaque core in the principal subject; coloured light stains selected edges rather than outlining everything.
- Make subject category, count, stance, and relationship readable at thumbnail size before adding texture.
- Give every composition one primary event and one governing vertical, horizontal, radial, curved, or diagonal axis.
- Confine pale convergence light to no more than `5%` of an identity frame.
- Apply multiscale particulate texture across expressive imagery, but remove it from text, icons, Focus, forms, data, consent, and Recovery.
- Keep interaction still by default; move only to clarify feedback, causality, orientation, or state.
- Build from named tokens, component recipes, and pattern rules before inventing choices.
- Use narrative objects, garments, animals, terrain, or architecture as optional modules, never as the identity itself.
- Never rely on colour, motion, texture, position, sound, or small text as the only carrier of required information.

Use full intensity for posters, covers, chapter openings, and editorial heroes. Use framed intensity for feature reveals and content thumbnails. Use restrained intensity for application shells. Use clean functional treatment for forms, data, checkout, settings, errors, consent, and other high-stakes tasks.

## Design DNA

Core territories:

- **Ember Void:** carbon darkness interrupted by a localized hot field.
- **Spectral Mineral:** desaturated cyan light that describes air, distance, or a counterplane.
- **Silhouette Ritual:** one or several dark masses organized by relationship and held in stillness.
- **Luminous Passage:** a curved or diagonal field that carries repeated forms into depth.

Emotional register:

The language is watchful, solemn, spacious, and unresolved. It can suggest danger, ceremony, passage, or wonder without threatening the user. Warmth feels consequential rather than cosy. Coolness feels spatial rather than clinical. Delight comes from recognition and controlled reveal, not bounce, confetti, noise, or surprise sound. Avoid novelty frontier, macho bravado, glossy luxury, horror gore, cyberpunk spectacle, cosy nostalgia, and sentimental melancholy.

Signature grammar:

- Carbon behaves as active material that conceals and isolates.
- Ember and mineral light collide through particles rather than a smooth gradient.
- Contour supplies recognition before face, anatomy, texture, or object detail.
- Edge light is lost and found; it reveals form while eroding its perimeter.
- One event dominates, one axis directs, and one pale intersection peaks.
- Sparse staging gives each object high narrative weight.
- Depth comes from scale reduction, overlap, haze, shadow, and route curvature.
- Fine grain appears at close range but never replaces compositional structure.
- Functional copy is terse, concrete, and placed on a calm support plane.
- State feedback is prompt and explicit while the wider field remains still.

Core principles:

1. **Start with the void.** Establish the field and its unoccupied region before subject, text, or light.
2. **Silhouette earns texture.** Prove Recognition in greyscale and at thumbnail size before adding colour or grain.
3. **Oppose temperature; refuse balance.** Both poles appear, but one owns the field.
4. **One event, one axis.** Additional detail must support the governing relationship.
5. **Reveal and erode.** Light exposes the important edge and particulate atmosphere interrupts completion.
6. **Use modules, not costume.** Contextual objects change; mass, light, space, and surface carry identity.
7. **Keep information clean.** Mood never obscures labels, data, Focus, terms, consent, or Recovery.
8. **Design at three distances.** Thumbnail, normal view, and close view must each reveal a different coherent layer.

Primary anti-patterns:

- Generic teal-and-orange cinematic grading.
- Smooth gradients, vector-clean outlines, uniform outer glow, or broad white fog.
- Equal warm/cool halves, rainbow accents, or multiple competing flares.
- Fully rendered faces and scenery inside otherwise simplified silhouettes.
- Fake film scratches, distressed paper, leather, wood, rust, sepia, or burned edges.
- “Wanted” lettering, costume icons, weapon metaphors, and compulsory frontier props.
- Dense Card walls, tiny controls, unlabeled navigation, decorative motion, or theatrical error states.

Outlier handling:

Weapons, hats, animals, shelters, bottles, vehicles, settlement forms, flora, and specific terrain are bounded narrative modules. They may support a literal, researched story, but they are never default requirements and cannot establish authenticity. Large landscapes and intimate figures are two valid scale families rather than competing styles. Either thermal pole may dominate, but equality is not allowed.

Establishment and subversion:

- Establish dark field, silhouette, thermal opposition, singular axis, and particulate surface before introducing novelty.
- Permit one major subversion per composition: invert thermal dominance or expand from iconic tableau to processional depth.
- Use roughly four to six establishment artifacts or states for every major subversion in a sequence.
- Return immediately to the core hierarchy after the high-impact moment.
- Reject any subversion that weakens legibility, accessibility, cultural safety, domain accuracy, or platform behavior.

## Reconstructive Fidelity

Canonical frame types:

1. **Iconic Vigil:** one large silhouette in the middle or lower field, with a single thin directional expectation.
2. **Luminous Gathering:** multiple dark brackets around one shared bright center and a radial axis.
3. **Paired Encounter:** two related vertical masses held in imperfect symmetry through gap, shadow, and thermal difference.
4. **Processional Passage:** repeated silhouettes diminishing along a curved or diagonal route into atmospheric depth.
5. **Functional Shell:** a carbon or matte light application surface with restrained accents, native components, and clean information islands.

Geometry, crop, and lighting:

- Identity plates begin at 3:4 portrait.
- Reserve roughly `42–50%` of the upper or expected-action region as a continuous calm dark field when the selected frame permits it.
- Keep the recognition core inside the central `72%` of width and `76%` of height for multi-ratio delivery.
- Place an iconic subject at `38–70%` of frame height; a gathering at `30–58%`; a procession begins with one mass at `18–36%` and decays rapidly.
- Perspective is economical. Use two or three planes for intimate scenes and strong scale diminution for routes.
- One thermal field may operate as ground, atmosphere, route, or local source. The opposite pole appears as a narrower rim, haze, or counterplane.
- A hard-enough dark core carries geometry. Local edge diffusion at a `736px` short-edge reference begins around `4–12px`; broad atmosphere around `12–40px`.
- Shadows are long and soft only when they reinforce the governing axis.
- Crop secondary atmosphere before the category-defining contour, bend, profile, wheel, horn, route turn, or interaction point.

Source-free reconstruction recipe:

1. Choose one canonical frame type and name its narrative purpose.
2. Create the target ratio and place the near-black field before any object.
3. Block the subject or relational cluster as one or more opaque greyscale silhouettes.
4. Downsample to thumbnail size; repair category, count, stance, and axis until they read.
5. Add the larger thermal territory, then the narrower opposing territory.
6. Attach a small pale convergence zone to the primary event.
7. Establish depth with overlap, scale, haze, route, and one shadow family.
8. Apply fine grain plus weaker low-frequency density variation; preserve the dark core.
9. Add live text and controls only on clean opaque support surfaces.
10. Check palette, type, composition, surface, motion, states, accessibility, crop, export, and drift.

Replication checks:

- Palette fidelity passes only when dark remains the majority, both thermal poles appear, their ownership is unequal, and pale convergence stays scarce.
- Type fidelity passes only when functional type is crisp, open, readable, and free from costume, glow, or texture.
- Spatial fidelity passes only when one event and one axis read before secondary detail and survive responsive crops.
- Surface fidelity passes only when dark structure remains intact under multiscale particulate light and functional content remains clean.
- Interaction fidelity passes only when feedback begins promptly, required states and Recovery are explicit, and Reduced motion preserves meaning.
- Reconstruction fidelity passes only when the subject can change completely while the language remains recognizable.

- Normal text targets `4.5:1`; qualifying large text and essential non-text graphics target `3:1` where applicable.
- State, alert, data, direction, and selection colour always have text, icon, shape, sign, pattern, or programmatic backup.

Mode behavior:

- Dark mode uses the full identity palette.
- Light mode uses matte parchment, carbon ink, darker warm/cool accents, and smaller expressive colour regions. A full identity plate may remain dark inside a light shell.
- High contrast uses system colours, solid boundaries, and no functional grain or bloom.
- Do not force dark mode. User and platform preferences take precedence.

Forbidden uses:

- Equal orange/cyan halves or a smooth complementary gradient.
- Broad neutral-white illumination.
- Ember as every error or mineral as every success without semantic backup.
- Muted teal or charcoal as small body text.
- Grain, opacity, blend mode, or glow used to repair failed text Contrast.

## Typography System

Type thesis:

Typography is quiet, open, and durable in product contexts, with rare grave editorial display. It acts as annotation and architecture, never as distressed atmosphere or historical costume.

Type roles:

| Role | Family | Weight | Size range | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Sturdy oldstyle/transitional serif or primary sans | `650–750` | `2.5rem–4.5rem` responsive | Short cover/chapter titles |
| Heading | Open-aperture humanist sans | `600–700` | `1.5rem–3rem` responsive | Section and screen hierarchy |
| Body | Open-aperture humanist sans | `400–500` | `1rem–1.25rem` | Reading, instruction, feedback |
| Label | Open-aperture humanist sans | `550–650` | `0.875rem–1rem` | Controls, navigation, metadata labels |
| Utility | Tabular mono, sparingly | `450–550` | `0.8125rem–0.9375rem` | Real structured values only |

Scale:

- Base: `16px` web-equivalent with user scaling.
- Hand-tuned steps: `13, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 32, 48, 72`.
- Display tracking: `-0.03em` to `0`.
- Body tracking: `0`.
- Label tracking: `0` to `0.02em`.
- Display line height: `0.96–1.06`.
- Heading line height: `1.10–1.22`.
- Body line height: `1.50–1.70`.
- Caption line height: `1.40–1.60`.
- Maximum ordinary body line: about `55–68` characters.

Microtype rules:

- Use sentence case. Reserve all caps for very short conventional abbreviations.
- Body weights remain `400+`; small text needs open apertures, distinct numerals, and durable counters.
- Use tabular numbers only where alignment matters.
- Allow at least `35%` localization expansion, flexible height, script fallbacks, RTL, and locale-aware formats.
- Enable optical sizing when the selected font supports it.
- Dark-mode type uses warm/mineral off-white without glow; review perceived weight and halation.
- Keep essential text live, selectable, searchable, translatable, and scalable.
- Avoid “wanted poster,” circus slab, rope, stencil, distressed, spur, condensed all-caps, faux small caps, and text filled with image texture.

## Spatial System

Space thesis:

An expressive field-and-axis composition sits beside an orderly 4px primitive and 8px ordinary grid. Negative space establishes importance; platform and task density remain functional.

Base unit: `4px`.

Spacing scale:

- `xs`: `4px`.
- `sm`: `8px`.
- `sm-plus`: `12px`.
- `md`: `16px`.
- `lg`: `24px`.
- `xl`: `32px`.
- `2xl`: `48px`.
- `3xl`: `64px`.
- `4xl`: `96px`.

Layout guidance:

- Compact viewport: `0–599px`; reduce secondary modules before the recognition core.
- Medium viewport: `600–1023px`; ordinary rhythm and two-plane layouts.
- Wide viewport: `1024–1439px`; expand the void and directional field.
- Cinematic viewport: `1440px+`; cap text measure and control sizes instead of scaling everything.
- Use a column grid appropriate to the application; consistency and task grouping matter more than a fixed universal column count.
- Controls use `12–16px` compact insets; panels `24–40px`.
- Touch targets remain independent from visible density.

Composition rules:

- One primary mass or relational cluster and one secondary directional cue.
- Center the mass for archetypal stillness; shift it off-axis only when the open side carries expectation.
- Use dark field, foreground occlusion, route, terrain, or light pool for containment. Avoid decorative borders.
- Use four z-bands: void `0`, atmosphere `10`, subject `20`, information `40`; temporary overlays begin at `60`.
- On wide crops, extend void or route. On narrow crops, remove peripheral repetitions and protect recognition contours.
- Dark masses feel heavier than their bounds; offset by `1–3%` toward the brighter/open side after optical review.
- Scene sparsity is not a rule for Tables, forms, or expert workflows.

## Surface And Material System

Surface thesis:

The language combines an absorptive matte carbon base with dry pigment, suspended mineral dust, chalky light, and friable chromatic edges. Functional surfaces remain clean and opaque.

Surface roles:

| Surface | Description | Implementation approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary field | Continuous carbon, visually flat and light-absorbing | `#0D0E0E`; no shadow; shell texture `0–1.5%` maximum |
| Atmospheric | Transparent coloured particles and low-frequency haze | Opacity about `8–36%`; behind/around subject; not a text surface |
| Subject core | Opaque dark mass with localized thermal contamination | Build contour first; apply edge effects through masks |
| Raised | Slightly lifted charcoal functional plane | Crisp boundary or restrained shadow; zero content texture |
| Overlay | Near-opaque carbon support for menus/dialogs/text | `92–100%` opacity; blocks crop-dependent contrast |
| Critical | Maximum-clarity information plane | Solid colour, tested Contrast, zero grain/bloom |

Light construction:

1. Carbon field and subject core.
2. One broad low-frequency thermal plane.
3. Narrower opposing rim or haze.
4. Small pale convergence at the primary event.
5. Edge diffusion and particulate continuity without lifting the whole shadow range.

Texture rules:

- Identity fine texture begins around `3–6%` luminance amplitude at the master scale.
- Combine mostly pixel-scale stipple with weaker `8–24px` density variation.
- Seed each asset and reject visible tiling or repeated speckle constellations.
- Increase chromatic particles near thermal boundaries while keeping the core mostly achromatic.
- Mask text, icons, Focus, data, diagrams, terms, and controls by at least `2px` at 1× and expand with scaling.
- Pre-render identity texture. Do not animate full-field noise or expensive blur at runtime.
- Preserve a high-quality master; inspect final chroma-subsampled JPEG at 100%.

Material vocabulary:

- Absorptive carbon.
- Dry mineral dust.
- Rubbed pigment.
- Chalk light.
- Coarse paper memory.
- Atmospheric residue.

Forbidden material:

- Glossy glass, wet reflection, chrome, plastic 3D, bevel, glassmorphism, neumorphism, leather, woodgrain, rust, film scratches, creases, torn edges, or deliberate compression damage.

## Motion System

Motion thesis:

Tense stillness dominates; fast functional feedback and slow optional reveal follow the composition’s axis without bounce, spectacle, or persistent drift.

Duration tokens:

- `instant`: `80ms` for press acknowledgment.
- `fast`: `120–180ms` for exit and compact feedback.
- `default`: `240–300ms` for disclosure and state transition.
- `axis`: `420ms` ceiling for larger directional product movement.
- `editorial`: up to `800ms` for optional, nonessential emergence.

Easing:

- Response: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`.
- Enter/reveal: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- Axis: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.75, 0.2, 1)`.
- Exit: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)`.

Motion rules:

- Feedback begins within `100ms`.
- Reveal field, subject core, luminous edge, then support content with no more than four staggered groups at `40–80ms`.
- Keep body text, Focus, critical alerts, subject recognition contours, and fine grain still.
- Preserve subject position and scale between related states.
- Use transform and opacity; avoid runtime animation of blur, colour filters, grain, or haze.
- Motion is interruptible and retargets from its current state.
- Exits are faster than entries.
- Loading uses a task label and determinate value when measurable.
- Error appears directly without shake. Success becomes a stable result without compulsory celebration.
- Persistent ambient motion is off by default and user-controllable when present.

Reduced motion:

- Replace axis travel with an immediate cut or opacity change under `100ms`.
- Replace atmospheric emergence with the static final frame.
- Replace processional animation with a static route and labelled sequence.
- Replace animated loader with a static icon plus task text or a restrained nonspatial indicator.
- Retain all state, hierarchy, causality, and Recovery.

Forbidden motion:

- Parallax, zoom-through-depth, camera shake, elastic spring, bounce, squash-and-stretch, flashing thermal fields, pulsing Focus, animated grain, looping route, or cinematic delay in repeated/high-stakes tasks.

## Interaction And States

Feedback density:

One action produces one immediate control response, one state-level message, and at most one optional nonvisual cue. The wider interface remains quiet. Feedback is never withheld for validation, save, payment, consent, permission, Loading, error, Completion, security, or Recovery.

State anatomy:

| State | Visual change | Copy behavior | Accessibility rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Default | Stable surface, readable label, visible affordance | Concrete noun or verb | Native role/name/state |
| Hover | One value, boundary, or underline change | Normally unchanged | Supplemental only; no exclusive information |
| Focus | Crisp solid ring with offset | Accessible name/description available | Keyboard-visible, independent of hover/selection/error |
| Active | Brief value change or `1–2px` compression | Normally unchanged | Commits once |
| Selected | Marker/check plus surface, shape, or weight | State remains visible | Programmatic Selected/current/pressed state |
| Disabled | Lower prominence but readable content | Explain prerequisite when useful | Native disabled state; no hover/press |
| Loading | Stable labelled progress | Name the task; add cancel/background context | Busy/progress semantics; avoid repeated announcements |
| Error | Icon/boundary/message beside affected scope | Problem, scope, and specific Recovery | Invalid/error association and appropriate announcement |
| Warning | Icon/boundary plus consequence | Risk, scope, reversibility, action | Redundant encoding and proportional interruption |
| Success | Stable completion mark and result | Name completed object and next step | Status semantics; no forced Focus move |

Silence rule:

Remove hover sound, idle animation, decorative particle response, repeated confirmation, and narration of obvious movement. Silence communicates composure, not uncertainty. Acknowledgment still begins promptly; longer work escalates to labelled Loading, progress, cancel, retry, or background continuation.

Trust rules:

- Preserve input, state, and Focus across transitions.
- Match confirmation strength to risk.
- Prefer undo for reversible actions.
- Distinguish waiting, queued, paused, offline, stale, failed, cancelled, unknown, and complete.
- Unknown outcome never maps to success.

## Component Recipes

### Scene Plate

Intent: host one identity-bearing event.

Anatomy: frame, absorptive field, atmosphere plane, subject/cluster, governing axis, optional convergence peak, optional support region.

Construction:

- Begin at 3:4 and place the recognition core within the crop-safe region.
- Keep dark structural area at `50–65%`.
- Select exactly one scene family.
- Bind warm and cool territories to specific planes or edges.
- Apply `3–6%` fine texture and weaker low-frequency variation.
- Keep functional content on the Scene Support Plate.

Variants: Iconic Vigil, Luminous Gathering, Paired Encounter, Processional Passage; warm-dominant or cool-dominant.

States: static default, optional one-time reveal, loading placeholder only when the media is genuinely pending, error with alt/context and retry outside the image.

Accessibility: purpose-based alt text; greyscale/CVD/thumbnail Recognition; Reduced motion static frame; no essential embedded text.

Forbidden: multiple scene families, automatic center crop, broad white light, full-edge glow, copied prop identity, or text over active grain.

### Silhouette Subject

Intent: carry subject category, count, stance, and relationship before detail.

Anatomy: opaque core, negative cuts, category landmarks, optional local hot rim, optional local cool rim, cast shadow.

Construction:

- Block in greyscale.
- Simplify until Recognition survives 10% size.
- Keep category landmarks separated.
- Add only localized edge contamination.
- Suppress facial and interior detail unless the use case requires identity or inspection, in which case use a different clean media treatment.

Variants: solitary, paired, clustered, repeated/processional; human, animal, object, architecture, landscape.

States: the image subject itself is noninteractive; interactive wrapping receives ordinary focus and labels.

Accessibility: never use anonymous silhouettes for real user identity, testimony, authorship, or representation.

Forbidden: uniform outline, shapeless fog, photoreal face pasted into a simplified body, or culturally specific costume used as generic atmosphere.

### Light Pool

Intent: reveal hierarchy and relate subjects through one luminous plane.

Anatomy: dominant thermal field, narrower counterfield, pale convergence peak, atmospheric spill, optional axis-aligned shadow.

Construction:

- Choose which thermal pole owns area.
- Attach the dominant field to ground, route, center, or one atmospheric plane.
- Bind the opposite pole to a rim or counterplane.
- Keep convergence below `5%`.
- Preserve dark subject cores and avoid neutral fill.

Variants: warm center/cool rim, cool field/warm rim, route, gathering center.

Accessibility: never use light temperature as the only status, direction, or relationship cue.

Forbidden: balanced split, smooth gradient, global colour grade, multiple peaks, or clipped white.

### Scene Support Plate

Intent: make copy and controls independent of image Contrast and crop.

Anatomy: opaque carbon or matte-light surface, live type, optional icon, actions, Focus boundary.

Construction:

- Use `92–100%` opacity.
- Remove grain, blur, glow, and chromatic shift.
- Apply ordinary typography, spacing, target, and component tokens.
- Position in a calm region but remain readable if the image is hidden.

Variants: caption, hero copy, chapter title, media controls.

States: standard component states defined above.

Accessibility: live text, semantic structure, validated Contrast, zoom/reflow, localization.

Forbidden: glass blur, gradient scrim, text shadow as primary repair, raster copy, or disappearing controls.

### Action Button

Intent: commit one named action.

Anatomy: target container, label, optional icon, Focus, optional progress.

Construction:

- Use component/semantic action tokens.
- One primary per local decision group.
- Label with verb plus object.
- Maintain `44×44 CSS px/pt` design target or `48×48dp` on Android, subject to stricter current guidance.
- Feedback begins within `100ms`.

Variants: primary, secondary, tertiary, destructive, icon where familiar.

States: Default, Hover, Focus, Active, Disabled, Loading, success/result, error/Retry.

Accessibility: native control; visible label for unfamiliar icon; keyboard/touch/screen-reader parity; no colour-only risk.

Forbidden: arbitrary colour variant, glow, texture, spinner-only Loading, or link behavior hidden in a Button.

### Recovery Message

Intent: explain failure or interruption and restore forward motion.

Anatomy: status icon, heading/message, affected scope, preserved-state note, Retry/alternate/escalation action.

Construction:

- Use a clean status surface.
- State fact, impact/scope, and next action.
- Preserve user input and completed work where safe.
- Match inline, banner, or dialog scope to severity.
- Keep the message durable when the outcome matters.

Variants: validation, network, offline/paused, permission, unknown outcome, support escalation.

States: visible error, retry Loading, success/complete, repeated failure/escalation.

Accessibility: programmatic association/announcement, keyboard actions, Focus management, no auto-dismiss for critical failure.

Forbidden: “Something went wrong” alone, colour-only error, dismissed error treated as success, or hidden Recovery.

## Prompt And Asset Rules

Prompt work starts from this manual and the routed source-free support files. Use `outputs/prompt-pack.md` for the complete executable contract.

Prompt intake must name:

- asset type, target surface, final use, audience, and channel;
- dimensions, ratio, safe areas, format, and transparency;
- exact rendered text or an explicit no-text rule;
- subject, count, relationship, axis, and focal event;
- measurable palette, crop, texture, and accessibility gates;
- cultural/safety risks, approval gates, destination, assumptions, and blockers.

Positive prompt facts:

- Tall portrait field or documented derived ratio.
- One iconic silhouette/cluster/route with exact count.
- Large active carbon void.
- Unequal ember-orange and mineral-cyan territories.
- Small pale convergence attached to the event.
- Broad low-frequency atmosphere plus multiscale pigment-like stipple.
- Lost-and-found illuminated perimeter around an intact dark core.
- Sparse scene with one governing axis and suspended narrative.

Negative constraints:

- No creator-name imitation or copied complete scene.
- No automatic frontier props, weapons, costume, animals, shelters, or landforms.
- No glossy 3D, vector outline, anime linework, neon cyberpunk, sepia, distressed paper, smooth gradient, broad white fog, multiple flares, pseudo-text, logo, or watermark.
- No uniform edge glow, over-rendered face, tiled noise, JPEG-like damage, or equal warm/cool split.

Variant rules:

- Change one purposeful variable at a time: subject, dominant temperature, environment, ratio, or scale family.
- Keep field, silhouette, axis, thermal inequality, peak scarcity, texture, and no-text constraint fixed unless the new intake changes them.
- For edits, name one target region, one operation, new visible state, and locked invariants.

Failure checks:

- Exact count and object separation.
- Subject/axis Recognition at thumbnail and in greyscale.
- Correct region binding for warm field, cool rim, peak, and shadow.
- Dark majority and peak ceiling.
- Grain quality, nonrepetition, and clean support areas.
- No extra text, motifs, objects, marks, or internal metadata.
- Crop, final encoding, accessibility, cultural safety, and usage permission.

Asset naming uses stable language, artifact, variant, and version terms, such as `emberglass-illustration-vigil-v01.png`.

## Accessibility

Web work targets WCAG 2.2 AA by default unless a stricter commitment or applicable platform/legal standard governs.

Contrast:

- Normal text: at least `4.5:1`.
- Qualifying large text: at least `3:1`.
- Essential non-text graphics/boundaries: at least `3:1` where applicable.
- Test final rendered pairs after compositing and in every state/mode.

Colour independence:

- Status uses icon, label, shape, and programmatic state.
- Selection uses marker/check and semantic state.
- Data uses sign, direct label, line/marker pattern, and exact value.
- Direction uses ordered label, arrow/position, and text.
- Identity media preserves subject and axis in greyscale.

Focus:

- Solid `2px` minimum perimeter with visible separation and preferred `4px` offset where geometry permits.
- Never glow, blur, animate, or clip Focus.
- Keep Focus distinct from Hover, Selected, error, and current.

Motion:

- Read Reduced motion before first render.
- Replace travel, parallax, ambient drift, and long fades with static hierarchy, cut, or short opacity change.
- Preserve state and causality.
- No more than three flashes per second within applicable thresholds; default to no flashing.

Targets and text:

- Formal web target-size compliance is evaluated against the chosen criterion; design target is `44×44 CSS px`, `44×44pt` on Apple platforms, `48×48dp` on Android, or stricter current guidance.
- Body text begins at `1rem` web-equivalent and weight `400+`.
- Support `200%` text resize, reflow at narrow equivalent width, native text scaling, `35%+` localization expansion, RTL, and complex scripts.

Alt text:

- Describe subject, action/relationship, setting, and function.
- Mention colour/texture only when meaningful.
- Do not infer identity, motive, culture, historical period, or authorship from a silhouette.

Media:

- Essential text remains live.
- Captions, transcript, audio description, playback controls, and static alternatives appear as required.
- Sound/haptics have simultaneous visual and semantic equivalents.

## Drift Boundaries

This system has drifted when any condition below is true:

- **Palette drift:** dark structure no longer owns the identity frame, one thermal pole is absent, warm/cool area is near-equal without purpose, or pale convergence exceeds `5%`.
- **Type drift:** functional text is distressed, condensed, glowing, textured, too light, unlabeled, unscalable, or styled as historical costume.
- **Spatial drift:** more than one event or axis competes, crop removes a recognition cue, or sparsity compromises task density/targets.
- **Surface drift:** silhouette core disappears, grain tiles or resembles compression, glossy material dominates, or texture enters functional information.
- **Motion drift:** decorative or persistent movement appears, feedback is delayed, motion is uninterruptible, or Reduced motion loses meaning.
- **Interaction drift:** Focus is missing/blurred, states rely on colour, Loading is unlabeled, error lacks Recovery, or unknown outcome becomes success.
- **Component drift:** a component bypasses semantic/component tokens, invents an unsupported variant/state, or ignores native Platform behavior.
- **Prompt drift:** a prompt uses vague mood alone, copies scene/creator identity, binds attributes incorrectly, adds pseudo-text/objects, or changes several variant axes at once.
- **Accessibility drift:** any required Contrast, Keyboard, Focus, target, text scaling, status, media alternative, CVD, or motion gate fails.
- **Cultural drift:** a narrative module becomes generic authenticity, stereotype, erased identity, or unsupported historical/cultural claim.

## Implementation Checklist

1. Name the use case, audience, channel, Platform, Domain, ratio, modes, and accessibility target.
2. Choose one canonical pattern and governing axis.
3. Build the greyscale field and silhouette before colour or texture.
4. Apply named semantic/component tokens rather than Raw values.
5. Keep dark majority, unequal thermal territories, and scarce convergence.
6. Place live information on a clean support surface.
7. Include all applicable states: Default, Hover, Focus, Active, Selected, Disabled, Loading, Error, Warning, Success, empty, offline, and stale.
8. Define validation, undo, Retry, resume, and Recovery.
9. Use native Platform navigation, forms, focus, dialogs, permissions, and back behavior.
10. Test dark, light, high contrast, Reduced motion, zoom/text scale, CVD, Keyboard, screen reader, touch, localization, RTL, offline/error, and responsive crops.
11. Inspect final identity media at thumbnail, normal size, close range, greyscale, bright environment, low brightness, and final encoding.
12. Use the prompt pack and failure checks for generated assets.
13. Confirm asset usage permission and cultural/domain review before production.
14. Run token drift and package validation.

## Review Rubric

| Dimension | Pass condition | Fail condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Language fidelity | One event, one axis, carbon majority, thermal inequality, silhouette, scarce peak, particulate edge | Generic dark/cinematic mood or prop-dependent imitation |
| Palette | Tokens/roles/proportions correct and accessible in context | Equal split, smooth grade, broad white, status colour alone |
| Typography | Quiet open functional type, rare grave display, readable scale/placement | Costume lettering, glow, texture, thin body, hidden labels |
| Space | Active void, Recognition core, responsive crop, task-appropriate density | Competing axes, lost contour, decorative Card wall, tiny controls |
| Surface | Matte carbon core, dry particulate light, multiscale texture, clean information | Gloss, uniform glow, tiled noise, texture behind content |
| Motion and states | Prompt feedback, stillness, complete states, Reduced-motion parity | Delay, bounce, persistent drift, missing state/Recovery |
| Components | Canonical recipe, semantic/component tokens, complete API and native behavior | Invented prop/variant, raw value, platform flattening |
| Prompt assets | Concrete facts, exact count/binding, one variant axis, negative constraints, failure checks | Vague style prompt, creator/scene copying, pseudo-text, extra objects |
| Accessibility | No blocker across Contrast, CVD, Focus, Keyboard, targets, text, motion, media | Any essential meaning unavailable or mode/state fails |
| Culture/domain | Contextual modules are necessary, researched, and bounded | Stereotype, false authenticity, unsafe metaphor, exact-data obstruction |

Score every dimension `pass`, `revise`, or `block`. An artifact is ready only when no dimension is `block` and all `revise` items have an accepted repair plan.
