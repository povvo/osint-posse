# Design Principles

Output path: `foundations/design-principles.md`

## Active Principles

| Principle | Operational rule | System layer controlled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start with the void | Establish the near-black field and its unoccupied region before placing subjects, text, or light | Palette, composition, modes, containers |
| Silhouette earns texture | Prove subject category, stance, and relationship at thumbnail size; add grain and internal colour only afterward | Imagery, iconography, components, prompts |
| Oppose temperature; refuse balance | Use warm and cool territories together with unequal area, unequal chroma, and a narrow convergence point | Palette, lighting, status adaptation |
| One event, one axis | Give each expressive surface one primary event and one governing vertical, horizontal, radial, curved, or diagonal gesture | Layout, crops, motion, scene recipes |
| Reveal and erode | Let light expose the important edge while particulate diffusion prevents a clean, complete outline | Surface, light, transforms, generated assets |
| Stillness carries consequence | Keep the field stable; move only to clarify feedback, causality, orientation, or state | Motion, interaction, loading |
| Use modules, not costume | Treat props, garments, landscapes, and period cues as optional narrative vocabulary, never as the identity itself | Imagery, brand expression, cultural review |
| Keep information clean | Remove grain, bloom, ambiguity, and theatrical delay from text, controls, focus, data, consent, and recovery | Typography, components, accessibility |
| Design at three perceptual distances | Verify icon at thumbnail, thermal hierarchy at normal size, and particulate craft at close range | Review, drift checks, reconstruction |
| Mood yields to meaning | When atmosphere conflicts with comprehension, platform convention, safety, or access, preserve meaning | Interaction, accessibility, localization |

## Emotional Register

- **Governing register:** watchful myth—sparse, solemn, materially atmospheric, and narratively unresolved.
- **Supporting registers:** ceremonial heat, mineral distance, quiet danger, communal gravity, vast passage, and restrained wonder.
- **Registers to avoid:** cosy nostalgia, novelty frontier, action spectacle, horror gore, neon futurism, glossy luxury, playful whimsy, macho aggression, and mournful sentimentality.
- **Tone test:** the work may imply danger without threatening the user; it may withhold narrative explanation without withholding functional guidance.

## The Establishment

- **Grammar being trained:** dark-majority field, silhouette-first recognition, unequal thermal polarity, one governing axis, scarce convergence light, particulate continuity, and sparse feedback.
- **How consistently it appears:** every identity-bearing artifact uses at least five of the seven cues. A functional surface may use fewer, but must retain carbon structure, restrained accents, clear hierarchy, and clean information.
- **Which foundations/components/patterns enforce it:** palette roles enforce thermal territory; scene and silhouette contracts enforce recognition; composition recipes enforce axis and density; surface transforms enforce grain and edge spill; interaction patterns enforce stillness and explicit feedback.
- **Recognition contract:** a viewer should be able to change the subject matter and still identify the language from mass, light, space, and texture.

## The Subversion

- **One rule that may break:** thermal area dominance may invert, or the normally centered iconic mass may become a deep processional route. Break only one of these at a time.
- **Trigger:** the narrative requires scale, migration, revelation, role reversal, or a decisive state change that cannot be expressed within the default tableau.
- **Maximum impact moment:** reserve the break for the hero, chapter turn, completion, or one editorial transition—not routine controls or every asset in a sequence.
- **Boundaries preventing chaos:** retain the dark majority, both temperature poles, silhouette recognizability, particulate continuity, and one governing axis. Never subvert accessibility, focus, state clarity, or recovery.
- **Return:** the following surface or state re-establishes the default hierarchy so the break remains legible as intentional.

## Establishment To Subversion Ratio

- **Minimum repetition before break:** establish at least three stable cues and one normal state before introducing a major variation in a sequence.
- **Frequency limit:** one major subversion per composition and roughly one per four to six related assets or states.
- **Recovery rule:** restore the dark structural field, singular focus, and clean information layer immediately after the high-impact moment.
- **Review threshold:** if the exception requires a paragraph to justify why it belongs, it is probably drift rather than productive subversion.

## Cross-Domain Coherence

| Domain | Principle expression |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Carbon majority, opposed ember/mineral territories, scarce pale convergence, no hue-only semantics |
| Typography | Quiet open-aperture infrastructure, rare grave display, low density, no costume lettering |
| Space | One event and axis, active void, two or three readable planes, portrait-first identity plates |
| Motion | Tense stillness, brief functional feedback, slow optional reveal, axis continuity, reduced-motion parity |
| Surface | Matte absorptive base, hard dark core, friable luminous edge, multiscale particulate finish |
| Interaction | Calm default, crisp focus, explicit state and recovery, interruption proportional to risk |
| Imagery | Contour recognition before detail, anonymity without dehumanization, modular subjects and settings |
| Accessibility | Clean support surfaces, redundant encoding, preserved zoom and focus, cultural and trauma review |

## Coherence Review

Ask these questions in order:

1. Does the artifact read as one event before it reads as a collection of details?
2. Is the dark field structurally necessary, or merely a dark background colour?
3. Can the subject survive without texture and at thumbnail size?
4. Are warm and cool territories relational and unequal?
5. Does the brightest region explain the hierarchy?
6. Is the texture structural yet absent from functional information?
7. Does motion clarify a state, or is it compensating for weak composition?
8. Could the narrative module change without losing the identity?
9. Can every user understand, operate, and recover from the result?

## Anti-Patterns

- Treating “dark,” “cinematic,” “gritty,” or “minimal” as sufficient principles without measurable rules.
- Copying surface motifs while ignoring figure-ground, thermal hierarchy, and perceptual scale.
- Making every artifact an exception, inverting multiple rules at once, or using subversion as a defence for incoherence.
- Protecting atmosphere at the expense of labels, focus, contrast, localization, recovery, or platform behavior.
- Equating the visual language with firearms, hats, bison, cacti, saloons, or any other narrative prop.
- Polishing away grain and edge instability, or adding grain to a composition with no strong structural core.
