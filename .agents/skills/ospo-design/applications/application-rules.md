# Application Rules

Output path: `applications/application-rules.md`

## Composition Rules

| Use case | Required foundations | Required components | Required patterns | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity poster or cover | Visual DNA, palette, spatial grammar, surface, imagery | Scene plate, silhouette subject, light pool | Iconic Vigil, Luminous Gathering, Paired Encounter, or Processional Passage | One scene family only; 3:4 master; no text inside active grain unless a clean support plate is used |
| Editorial chapter opening | Typography, imagery, voice, motion | Scene plate, scene support, navigation link | One scene pattern plus Quiet Disclosure | Title may be evocative; orientation and next action remain explicit |
| Wide landing hero | Palette, responsive space, typography, imagery | Scene plate, support plate, primary/secondary Button | Iconic Vigil or Processional Passage, Clean Support | Extend void/route for width; do not crop recognition core or place CTA on unstable imagery |
| Mobile story/vertical media | Spatial grammar, crop rules, motion, accessibility | Scene plate, captions/media controls | Iconic Vigil or Luminous Gathering | Protect upper/lower platform UI, safe text, captions, reduced-motion/static version |
| Social 1:1 or 4:5 asset | Palette, imagery, brand expression | Derived scene crop | Singular Axis, Silhouette Anchor | Recompose from master metadata; do not automatic-center-crop away the directional cue |
| Product/application shell | Typography, iconography, interaction, modes | Native shell, navigation, forms, panels, status | Clean Support, Native Shell | Atmosphere is limited to entry/chapter/media regions; repeated tasks remain crisp and platform-native |
| Feature reveal | Visual DNA, motion, voice | Scene plate, support plate, action | Iconic Vigil plus Quiet Disclosure | Reveal clarifies hierarchy; never delay the action or replay on every visit |
| Editorial collection | Imagery rhythm, typography, spatial grammar | Content Cards/list, scene thumbnails | Alternate intimate and processional patterns | Avoid identical centered silhouettes or the same prop across every item |
| Empty state | Voice, iconography, accessibility | Empty-state panel, action, optional compact illustration | Clean Support plus optional Silhouette Anchor | Low emotional severity; explain absence and next action; no loneliness/danger metaphor |
| Guided sequence | Spatial grammar, interaction, motion | Step indicator, forms, progress, actions | Processional Passage may supplement explicit Sequence Progress | Route imagery is decorative; labelled Steps, save, resume, and Recovery are primary |
| Data/finance/operational view | Typography, colour accessibility, density | Tables, charts, filters, status | Native Shell, Clean Support | No grain/bloom on data; brand warm/cool is not a categorical/status palette |
| High-stakes form or transaction | Typography, voice, interaction, accessibility | Forms, review, dialog, receipt, Recovery | Clean Support, Explicit Recovery, Stable Completion | Zero decorative texture/motion; complete consequence, consent, audit, and error handling |
| Product or food inspection | Imagery accuracy, accessibility | Accurate media gallery, metadata, zoom | Clean Support | Identity image may frame the story but cannot replace accurate colour/condition/doneness views |
| Audio/video cover and player | Imagery, motion, sound, accessibility | Scene cover, native media controls, transcript/captions | One scene family, Native Shell | Controls and captions sit on stable support; no atmospheric autoplay |
| Print poster/editorial plate | Palette, surface, typography, composition | Scene master, vector/live type | One scene family | Soft-proof CMYK/gamut, scale grain to final size, retain a high-quality master, keep type live |
| Presentation title/divider | Visual DNA, typography, spatial grammar | Scene plate, support title | Iconic Vigil or Processional Passage | One identity plate per section; content slides use clean hierarchy and charts |

## Application Intensity

| Level | Where | Identity cues | Functional treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full | Poster, cover, chapter opening, campaign hero | All seven core cues | Minimal controls on clean support |
| Framed | Landing section, editorial Card, feature reveal | Four to six cues in one media region | Ordinary product components beside/under media |
| Restrained | Product shell, navigation, content collection | Carbon/thermal restraint, typography, spacing, optional small media | Native functional components, zero text texture |
| Functional | Forms, data, checkout, consent, errors, settings | Quiet voice, restrained palette, clean hierarchy | Clarity and platform convention dominate |
| High contrast | Accessibility/system preference | Structural hierarchy and semantic names only | System colours, solid boundaries, zero atmospheric effects |

## Adaptation Rules

- **Platform adaptation:** choose native Navigation, form, feedback, permission, focus, haptic, and back behavior first. Map semantic tokens and identity media into that shell; do not reproduce Web chrome on native platforms.
- **Domain adaptation:** adopt domain nouns, units, status, regulation, accuracy, and safety. Use narrative modules only after cultural/rights review and only when they support the actual communication goal.
- **Density adaptation:** sparse identity composition does not require sparse Tables, dashboards, lists, or expert workflows. Keep touch targets, readable text, focus, and grouping regardless of density.
- **Theme adaptation:** dark is the strongest identity mode; light uses matte parchment and compact darker accents. Full identity media may remain dark inside a light shell. High contrast uses system colours; Reduced motion uses static hierarchy.
- **Channel adaptation:** editorial channels may carry full grain/light. Product, email, documents, and notifications use restrained flat versions. Never embed essential text in generated/raster imagery.
- **Aspect adaptation:** derive crops from recognition-core, governing-axis, safe-text, and focal metadata. Expand void or route before enlarging/cropping the subject.
- **Sequence adaptation:** alternate scale, axis, count, and dominant temperature; retain at least five core identity cues and permit one major subversion at a time.

## Asset Construction Order

1. Name the Use case, audience, channel, domain, and target ratios.
2. Select one scene or functional pattern.
3. Build greyscale field, silhouette, and axis; test thumbnail Recognition.
4. Add unequal thermal territories and a scarce convergence point.
5. Add depth, particulate edge, and full-field texture.
6. Add clean support surfaces, live text, controls, and accessibility alternatives.
7. Produce platform/channel variants from the same composition metadata.
8. Validate final encoding, crop, contrast, CVD, localization, Reduced motion, cultural context, and rights.

## Channel-Specific Constraints

- **Email:** assume images may be blocked; use live HTML text, alt text, high-contrast CTA, and a restrained static crop.
- **Documents/PDF:** keep searchable/selectable live text, tagged structure, alt text, logical reading order, and print-safe image resolution.
- **Presentation:** use identity scenes for titles/dividers; keep body slides clean and readable at distance.
- **Social:** never rely on caption text baked into art; protect platform overlays and supply accessible post text.
- **App store/marketplace:** follow current platform specifications and show truthful product experience, not only atmospheric campaign art.
- **Large display:** scale grain spatially and cap text measure/control size; do not enlarge everything with viewport width.

## Anti-Patterns

- Applying full atmospheric treatment to every screen, Card, icon, chart, email, and document page.
- Using one 3:4 image with automatic crops for every channel.
- Treating dark mode as mandatory identity or light mode as a simple inversion.
- Styling a functional Domain pattern before defining exact data, state, errors, and Recovery.
- Reusing the same scene prop until it becomes the identity.
- Hiding live text, labels, terms, or status in raster/generated art.
