## Recognition And Hierarchy

Use a three-distance test:

1. **Thumbnail:** Recognition comes from the dark mass, subject relationship, and governing axis. If category or count disappears, simplify contour before adding light.
2. **Normal viewing:** Hierarchy comes from thermal area, focal convergence, scale, and negative space. The eye should land on one event, then one directional cue.
3. **Close viewing:** material interest comes from stochastic grain, chromatic particles, edge diffusion, and restrained internal variation. Close detail must not overturn the first two readings.

The information layer uses the same hierarchy in cleaner form: one primary task, explicit headings/labels, restrained accents, and low-noise support surfaces.

## Pattern Families

### Brand Palette

- Carbon is structural, not merely a dark theme.
- Ember and mineral families are relational; object-specific colour assignment is discouraged.
- Pale convergence is scarce and attached to hierarchy.
- Status meaning remains semantic and redundant rather than inheriting atmospheric colour directly.

### Typography Style

- Functional typography is open, sturdy, and quiet.
- Rare editorial display introduces gravity without costume lettering.
- Hierarchy uses scale, weight, spacing, and placement before decorative colour.
- Text remains crisp and untextured.

### Icon Style

- Utility icons are familiar, low-detail, and crisp.
- Fill may indicate selected state; atmospheric duotone belongs only to large expressive marks.
- Metaphors are literal and culturally reviewed; unfamiliar actions retain labels.

### Imagery And Illustration Direction

- Hero: maximum use of void, thermal asymmetry, silhouette, and particulate edge.
- Product: accurate inspection imagery remains clean; identity treatment frames rather than obscures it.
- Editorial: intimate iconic plates alternate with processional vistas.
- Avatar: real identity uses real/user-chosen representation or initials, never anonymous scene silhouettes.
- Empty state: one compact low-severity cue plus explicit explanation/action.
- Background: one restrained field with a protected low-texture information zone.

### Layout Density And Shape

- Identity scenes are sparse; tasks use domain-appropriate density.
- Shapes are restrained, mostly square to softly rounded, with containment expressed through field and space.
- Cards and panels are functional grouping devices, not universal decoration.
- Negative space protects hierarchy without shrinking touch targets or reducing scanning efficiency.

### Motion Language

- Stillness is the default.
- Feedback is immediate; larger transitions decelerate along the governing axis.
- Ambient drift is off by default and removed in reduced motion.
- A static state cue always preserves meaning.

### Voice, Tone, And Copy

- Voice is spare, observant, concrete, and humane.
- Task copy states action, object, consequence, and Recovery.
- Editorial language may remain unresolved only after functional meaning is complete.
- Genre slang, bravado, and culturally loaded metaphors are excluded.

## Consistency Contract

Consistency is relational, not identical repetition:

- Every identity artifact uses at least five of these seven cues: carbon majority, both thermal poles, silhouette recognition, one event, one axis, scarce convergence, particulate continuity.
- A functional surface may use fewer atmospheric cues but retains carbon/light hierarchy, restrained accent, clean information, explicit state, and quiet motion.
- Only one major subversion—thermal dominance inversion or processional expansion—appears in a composition.
- Subject matter, crop, dominant temperature, and narrative module may vary without changing the underlying perception sequence.
- When platform convention conflicts with a surface cue, preserve semantic purpose and perceptual restraint rather than identical chrome.

## Perceptual Review

- Squint/blur: one event and axis remain.
- Greyscale: subject and hierarchy remain without warm/cool dependence.
- Protan/deutan/tritan simulation: status and essential relationships remain through labels/shape.
- Thumbnail: contour category survives.
- High contrast/forced colours: functional state remains clear after grain and bloom disappear.
- Close inspection: grain is stochastic, multiscale, and masked away from information.
- Sequence review: intimate and wide plates vary; repeated assets do not become a template stamp.
- Cultural review: narrative modules communicate the intended context without stereotype, false authenticity, or erased identity.

## Anti-Patterns

- Describing the language only as “dark, cinematic, gritty, orange and teal.”
- Smooth complementary gradients, uniform grain filters, full-edge glows, or broad white bloom.
- Equal focal weight, multiple axes, busy props, or atmosphere substituted for content hierarchy.
- Costume lettering, frontier-themed icons, repeated weapons/animals/landforms as brand identifiers.
- Sparse task layouts that reduce usability, dense hero art that loses the void, or Cards around everything.
- Perceptual Consistency judged by identical colours while focus, typography, state, motion, or cultural meaning drifts.
