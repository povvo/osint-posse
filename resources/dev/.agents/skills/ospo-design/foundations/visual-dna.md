# Visual Design DNA

Output path: `foundations/visual-dna.md`

## Core Visual Territory

- **Named territories:** Ember Void, Spectral Frontier, Silhouette Ritual, and Luminous Passage.
- **Boundary of the territory:** nocturnal or light-starved scenes in which a few dark, recognizable masses are revealed by opposed hot and cold illumination. The language is atmospheric illustration, not clean vector art, conventional photography, sepia nostalgia, neon cyberpunk, or literal historical reconstruction.
- **Primary visual forces:** light-absorbing negative space; ember-orange versus mineral-cyan polarity; dark structural silhouettes; pale, short-lived convergence light; softened chromatic rims; particulate diffusion; sparse, iconic staging.
- **What must be recognizable at a glance:** one dominant event, its directional axis, and the subject category. Recognition comes from contour, stance, spacing, and scale before interior detail.

## Dominant Emotional Register

- **Register name:** watchful myth.
- **Emotional temperature:** externally hot and internally cool; solemn rather than sentimental.
- **Tension pattern:** prolonged stillness charged by one directional cue—a gaze, path, prop, plume, shadow, or procession.
- **Trust, threat, delight, calm, or melancholy cues:** threat is implied through occlusion and distance; calm through spacious staging; melancholy through isolated masses; wonder through disproportionate light. Delight is rare and muted. The system should feel observant, not aggressive.

## Repeated Grammar

| Grammar signal | How it repeats | Where it controls the system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black as active material | Large near-black fields frame, conceal, and absorb detail | Foundation surfaces, scene containers, mode tokens |
| Unequal thermal polarity | Hot and cold colour families coexist, but one owns more area | Palette roles, light recipes, status adaptation |
| Silhouette before detail | Subjects remain readable as filled masses at thumbnail scale | Imagery, icons, figures, component hierarchy |
| Chromatic edge contamination | Light stains the perimeter instead of outlining it cleanly | Surface transforms, generated assets, focus treatment |
| Particulate continuity | Fine multiscale grain crosses every value region | Texture overlays, media processing, drift checks |
| Sparse ceremonial staging | Few elements carry high narrative weight around a void or luminous center | Layout, density, copy restraint, interaction register |
| One governing axis | A vertical, horizontal, radial, or curved cue organizes each composition | Composition recipes, motion choreography, responsive crops |
| Atmospheric depth | Scale reduction, haze, overlap, and luminous paths imply space without fine perspective detail | Scene components, landscape variants, transition layers |

## Invariants

- Reserve a genuinely dark structural field; charcoal-grey convenience backgrounds are insufficient.
- Use both warm and cool chromatic families in every hero expression, with visibly unequal area.
- Preserve a dark core inside principal subjects. Do not turn them into uniformly glowing figures.
- Make the silhouette legible before adding texture, bloom, or internal colour.
- Limit each frame or surface to one dominant visual event and one secondary directional cue.
- Keep edges optically soft but compositionally decisive.
- Apply grain as a structural finish across the whole field, not as a decorative filter on isolated objects.
- Allow a narrow pale convergence zone; broad neutral-white illumination destroys the thermal tension.

## Allowed Variation

- Either warm or cool illumination may dominate the field.
- Composition may shift between iconic portrait, central gathering, paired encounter, and processional vista.
- Subjects may be human, animal, object, landscape, or abstract atmosphere if their contour survives detail suppression.
- Narrative modules—garments, headwear, tools, shelters, vessels, vehicles, flora, architecture, or terrain—may change by domain and culture.
- Grain density, diffusion radius, and halo size may scale with output size, but their relative hierarchy must remain.
- Support text and UI may use crisp geometry for accessibility, provided it is placed in calm dark regions and does not imitate the image texture.

## Contradictions To Preserve

- Graphic silhouettes must coexist with painterly dissolution.
- The field should feel both empty and narratively loaded.
- Light must reveal form while also eroding its edge.
- Warmth should feel dangerous or ceremonial, not cosy; coolness should feel spatial, not clinical.
- Staging may appear primitive or timeless while production remains deliberate and controlled.
- Stillness carries implied movement; animation should reveal latent direction without making the world busy.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic orange-and-teal cinematic grading without a near-black structural field.
- Smooth gradients, clean airbrushed bloom, vector-sharp outlines, or uniform film-noise overlays.
- Sepia heritage styling, leather-and-wood clichés, novelty “cowboy” decoration, or compulsory weapons.
- Fully rendered faces, photoreal skin, polished 3D materials, glossy neon, or dense environmental detail.
- Equal warm/cool splits, rainbow accents, broad white light, or multiple competing focal flares.
- Busy grids, card walls, verbose copy, persistent motion, bouncy easing, or feedback that breaks the watchful stillness.
- Reducing recognizability until subjects become shapeless fog.

## Reconstruction Target

A successful reconstruction reads in three passes: at thumbnail size, a dark iconic event and its governing axis; at normal size, unequal hot/cold fields and atmospheric depth; at close range, chromatic particulate texture and softened edge spill. Remove any one pass and the artifact should feel incomplete. A new work belongs when its subject can change entirely while these three perceptual passes remain intact.
