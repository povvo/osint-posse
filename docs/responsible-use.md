# Responsible use

OSINT Posse structures an operator’s work; it does not supply authority to begin
it. Before collection, record the purpose, lawful basis or other authority,
scope, accountable decision-maker, retention period, and the least intrusive
method likely to answer the question.

## Required boundaries

- Collect public or properly authorised material only.
- Minimise people, attributes, sources, and retention to the stated purpose.
- Record negative decisions and uncertainty, not only useful-looking results.
- Require human review before an inference affects a person.
- Provide correction, redaction, and deletion routes where applicable.
- Stop when collection becomes disproportionate, unlawful, unsafe, or outside
  the approved scope.

Do not use OSINT Posse for stalking, harassment, doxxing, coercion, discriminatory
targeting, unlawful surveillance, credential theft, access-control bypass, or
the exploitation of children or vulnerable people.

Do not use its output as the sole or automated basis for decisions about
employment, housing, credit, insurance, education, healthcare, immigration,
benefits, policing, sentencing, or another high-impact service.

## Optional profiling

Psychological or behavioural profiling is disabled in the normal workflow.
Enabling it requires a separate Case Decision Log entry recording purpose,
authority, necessity, subjects, permitted outputs, retention, review owner, and
explicit operator approval. Profile statements remain contestable inferences;
they are not diagnoses, proof of intent, or predictions of conduct.

Documentation makes this boundary reviewable. It does not enforce the boundary
for the operator, which remains an inconveniently human-shaped dependency.
